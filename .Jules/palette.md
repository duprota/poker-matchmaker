## 2026-06-30 - Added tooltips and aria-labels to GameHeader buttons
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons often lack descriptive names, causing accessibility issues for screen readers and usability confusion for users. The GameHeader component had several such buttons.
**Action:** Applied 'title' and 'aria-label' attributes systematically to all icon-only buttons (Back, Save, Cancel, Edit, Delete) in GameHeader to ensure native tooltips and screen reader support.
